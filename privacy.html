Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What we collect

Your phone number for sign-in, and the property, unit, tenant, contract, payment, cheque and expense records that a business owner or their staff enter. We do not collect location or contacts.

How we use it

Only to run the app: show accounts, calculate arrears and profit shares, and send a sign-in code when you request one. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Each business sees only its own data. A tenant sees only their own account; an heir sees only their own share. Data is stored with Supabase; sign-in codes are delivered through Twilio (SMS or WhatsApp).

Deleting your account

Delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account) or email us and we will delete it.
